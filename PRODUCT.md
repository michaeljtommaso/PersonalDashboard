# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Michael Tommaso, sole user. Uses this daily at a desktop or laptop, morning through evening, as a command center for a structured and ambitious summer. The context is focused and intentional: he opens this to check progress, log work, and make decisions, not to browse. No external users, no onboarding flow needed.

## Product Purpose

A personal operating system for summer 2026 (May 19 through August 31). Tracks health (gym splits, nutrition), business ventures (revenue, lessons learned), academic prep (Cornell E&M CASE exam), AI agent operations, and daily knowledge capture. Success means opening it every morning feels motivating, and closing it at night reflects real progress made.

## Brand Personality

Ambitious, premium, composed. The energy of someone building something serious, expressed through restraint rather than noise. Bold where it matters (numbers, progress, status), quiet everywhere else.

## Anti-references

- **Generic SaaS dashboard**: no hero-metric templates, no identical icon-grid cards, no purple gradient blobs, no decorative data visualizations that don't say anything
- **Hacker/terminal aesthetic**: not green-on-black, not monospace-as-personality, not fake CLI vibes
- **Notion clone**: not white-space-as-design, not block-editor metaphors, not soft pastel neutrals

Reference feeling: Vercel and Stripe dashboard. Ultra-clean, data-forward, confident typography, high trust. The interface steps back and lets the data speak.

## Design Principles

1. **Data earns attention.** Only surface numbers and states that inform a real decision. Decorative metrics are noise.
2. **Ambitious but composed.** Bold accent color and scale create energy. Structured layout keeps it from feeling chaotic. Both must coexist.
3. **Tool, not toy.** Every interaction is purposeful. No gamification, no confetti, no badge unlocks, no reward loops.
4. **Sections feel distinct, system feels coherent.** Each module has its own accent color and visual identity. The underlying grid and typography unify them.
5. **Premium by restraint.** The dark palette and typography do the heavy lifting. Ornament is the enemy.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Personal use only. No external users, no compliance requirements. Color contrast should be comfortable for long sessions (dark room, evening use) but no formal WCAG target required.
